Our Purpose

Built to keep campus life visible and welcoming.

Eventify started as a web development project to replace scattered bulletin-board flyers and buried email newsletters with one calm, dependable event calendar.

Every semester, student clubs, academic departments, and community organizers host dozens of thoughtful workshops, lectures, concerts, and study showcases. Too often, students hear about them the morning after they happen simply because announcements are fragmented across different platforms.

Eventify brings those listings together in an editorial, distraction-free format. Instead of noisy social feeds or complicated ticketing checkouts, every listing gives you the essentials right away: what the session covers, when and where it takes place, and a single straightforward form to save your spot.

Developed with clean HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, the site keeps registration records directly in your browser’s local storage so students can manage their passes quickly without creating another password or account.
